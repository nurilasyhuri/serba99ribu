# UI brief

## Niche
Curated affiliate e-commerce and shopping guide for Indonesian value shoppers, focused exclusively on tested, high-utility products priced at or under Rp 99.000 sourced from 99ribu.com and top marketplace stores.

## Audience
Smart Indonesian mobile shoppers, bargain hunters, and home/lifestyle enthusiasts looking for verified quality items under Rp 99.000 without wading through low-quality marketplace junk. They trust clear photos, honest descriptions, real prices, and transparent outbound merchant destinations.

## Primary job
Enable users to quickly discover, filter, search, and click through curated affiliate products and shoppable articles with zero latency and zero friction.

## Primary action
Outbound affiliate click-through to verified merchants ("Beli di 99ribu" / "Cek Promo 99ribu") with integrated affiliate tracking.

## References wanted
- `https://99ribu.com` (Direct structural, layout, and ergonomic model: omnibox header, category pills, high-density product cards, fast mobile navigation).

## Non-goals
- No internal shopping cart, no guest/user checkout flow, no payment gateway or COD logistics handling (delegated 100% to merchant).
- No distracting AI slop, no fake countdown timers, no animated popups or intrusive spam banners.
