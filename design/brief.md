# UI Brief — serba99ribu.com

## Niche
Curated affiliate e-commerce and lifestyle shopping guide featuring vetted, high-utility home, fashion, beauty, kitchen, and automotive lifestyle products capped at or under Rp 99.000.

## Audience
Discerning Indonesian mobile and desktop shoppers looking for affordable yet modern, stylish, and high-utility lifestyle products. They appreciate calm editorial lookbooks, transparent flat pricing (≤ Rp 99.000), verified packshots, and clean outbound links to official/verified merchants without tacky discount traps.

## Primary job
Provide high-velocity, aesthetically elevated visual discovery of curated products under Rp 99.000 through luxury video ambient hero, category quick pills, curated department grids, and lifestyle editorial buying guides.

## Primary action
Outbound affiliate discovery click-through to verified partner merchant pages ("Lihat Koleksi" / "Beli di Mitra Resmi").

## References wanted
- `https://id.louisvuitton.com/` (Louis Vuitton: atmospheric full-bleed video hero, understated luxury typography, generous breathing room, calm pacing).
- `https://themes.shopify.com/themes/metro/` (Metro Shopify Theme: architectural category cards, story collection pills, high-density yet elegant cards, editorial guides).

## Non-goals
- No internal shopping cart, checkout, or payment gateway handling (pure affiliate / curated discovery).
- No spammy countdown clocks, fake social proof popups, or harsh neon discount stickers.
- No direct raw database dumps without category grouping and curation.
